Meridian Docks — a round-based tactical FPS in the browser on three.js. Two teams fight over a dusk harbour: the Tidebreakers attack and plant the surge charge at Site A (Crane Yard) or Site B (Container Row), the Harbour Watch defends. Money earned each round buys rifles, SMGs, a DMR and grenades; gunfire rewards stopping to aim — recoil and spread punish firing on the move, headshots pay. Single-player against nine bots that walk lanes, hold angles, react to sight and sound, plant and defuse. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make the best three.js tactical FPS” bounty, $1,000 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080 — click to play
```

Controls: WASD move · mouse look · click fire · right mouse aim · R reload · Ctrl crouch · Space jump · Q / 1–3 switch weapon · E plant the charge (hold, inside a site) · G frag · H smoke · B buy · M mute. First to 4 rounds takes the harbour.
